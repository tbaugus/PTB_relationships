Nine books, rewritten

In September 2026 NWCG republished nine Position Task Books that had been in the legacy format (most since June 2009) as Next Gen editions. This page lays each book's old task list beside its new one, task by task, so the scale of the change is visible before anyone relies on the new edition.

What a rewrite means

The nine books did not get edited; they were re-authored in NWCG's Next Gen template. That template changes what a task book is, which is why so little text carries over.

Before: legacy format

  • Tasks grouped under Behavior: competency headings (8 to 26 per book).
  • Sub-bullets under tasks explain and clarify; they are not evaluated.
  • Completion is evaluator initials and a date per task; no rating scale.
  • One code letter per task; detail lives inside the book.

After: Next Gen format

  • Tasks sit in a position evaluation table under 6 to 9 standard categories (Prepare and Mobilize, Build the Team, Manage Risk, Demobilize ...).
  • Each task carries an evaluation-context code (I, W, RX, R, S, O, ALL) and is rated N/O, D, M or E per assignment.
  • Explanatory detail moves to the companion Incident Position Standards (PMS 350-xx). For STAM, GISS and DECK that standard is not published yet.
  • A shared leadership-level task opens every book and many "Build the Team" and "Manage Risk" tasks are now word-identical across books.
